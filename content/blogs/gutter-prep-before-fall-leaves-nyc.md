Late August is deceptively close to peak leaf season in New York City. By the time trees along the block start turning color, gutters that were clear all summer can fill up within a couple of weeks — and a clogged gutter system is one of the most common, and most preventable, causes of roof and siding damage each fall.

At **Neat Services Corp**, this is the point in the year we encourage homeowners to get ahead of the rush. Once leaves start dropping in earnest, gutter cleaning schedules book up fast, and a missed cleaning often means water finds its way somewhere it shouldn't.

## Why Gutters Matter More Than Homeowners Think

Gutters and downspouts exist to move water away from the roof edge, fascia, and foundation. When they're blocked by leaves and debris, water has nowhere to go but over the edge, which can lead to fascia rot, siding stains, and water pooling right at the roofline — exactly where leaks tend to start.

Homes near mature trees, common throughout Queens, Brooklyn, and the Bronx, are especially at risk once leaf drop begins in earnest.

## 3 Steps to Take Before Leaf Season Peaks

1. **Clear gutters now, before the heaviest drop:** a late-summer cleaning gets ahead of the volume that's coming in the following weeks.
2. **Check for loose or sagging gutter sections:** hangers can loosen over a summer of heat and storms, and a sagging gutter won't drain properly even when it's clear.
3. **Inspect the roofline for early signs of water backup:** staining along the fascia or roof edge can indicate water has already been overflowing.

## Why Timing Matters

Waiting until leaves are already piling up means cleaning gutters that will just refill within days. Getting ahead of the season — clearing debris now and confirming the system is draining properly — means your gutters are ready to handle the heaviest weeks of leaf drop without backing up.
